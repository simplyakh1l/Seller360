# Recording a Seller360 walkthrough

Use the production build on localhost, with microphone access allowed. On a small laptop stop the development command before using the production server. Prepare the speech model once before recording: its first download and local model startup can exceed a short demo slot. Do not imply the current local setup reliably completes a conversation within 90 seconds.

1. Open Dashboard. Show the sales comparison, orders, unchanged ASP and operating evidence. Briefly show Data & privacy to establish that the supplied figures are illustrative, undated monthly totals with no marketplace connection.
2. Select Get help from Nova, then Start a voice call. Wait for the introduction before stating a concern. No thinking cue should play before the greeting.
3. Say “Why did my sales fall? What should I investigate first?” Let Nova finish. The live screen shows the call state, optional captions and controls; the full transcript appears after the call.
4. Give truthful scenario context, then ask for a plan if useful. Check generated explanations against the observed totals; monthly aggregates cannot prove root causes.
5. Review the proposed tasks before agreeing to save them locally. Open Actions afterward to demonstrate real start/complete/reopen state changes. No marketplace operations occur.
6. End the call and open Saved calls within Nova to revisit its summary and transcript. Suggestions is the secondary view inside Actions.

For macOS recording use Shift–Command–5 and verify the selected capture method records both microphone and system audio. Voice quality depends on installed system voices, browser recognition availability, microphone conditions and local resources. Use English or Hindi as appropriate; do not present a prepared transcript as spontaneous recognition.
